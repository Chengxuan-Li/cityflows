# 2026-11-22 — Airports: choice of airports and batch plan

Owner brief (chat, 2026-11-22): a new feature set, airports. A bundled real place with a whole airport
inside it (ideally more than one detached terminal and more than one runway, crossing runways preferred),
starting from a simple one-runway airport as regression; a baseline model of the area; special handling of
runways, taxiways and gates, topologically correct first and visually second; aircraft as a vehicle with no
human journeys (like trains and trams); departures, arrivals, taxiing, gate handling, remote stands,
maintenance and hangars, and moves between them for irregular and maintenance operations; seven aircraft
classes (large double-decker, widebody, narrowbody, regional, propeller, private, helicopter); visuals and
lighting coordinated with landing, departure and aircraft movement; runway and taxiway navigation under
changing runway directions; gate limits and runway and taxiway limits by aircraft category. The batch runs
unsupervised; the lead sends screenshots as steps land and does not stop for confirmation.

## Owner decisions

- Airports, in order: **London City (LCY)** first, the small real one with the best OSM quality; then a
  **synthetic one-runway airport** built to that schema; then **Zurich (ZRH)**, the large target; later
  **Boston (BOS)** as the crossing-runway complexity test and **Heathrow (LHR)** as the operational-scale test.
- Downloads approved: the small real, the large real and the later two, with perimeter roads and facilities
  outside the aerodrome boundary where they help realism and road routing.
- Contract changes: "any change needed" (schema, archives, `docs/architecture.md`).
- Operations: a seeded daily schedule sized to the airport's real volume and stand mix, with turnarounds,
  tows to remote stands and hangars; runway direction from time-of-day concepts plus a wind control in the UI
  that triggers a reconfiguration.

- Clarifications (2026-11-22): airport editing is a later task (TASK-305, after Zurich), import-only until then;
  road vehicles on airside service roads give way to aircraft at taxiway crossings, and flights generate no
  landside trips; aircraft are shown on about 10 km of approach and climb beyond the map edge.

At most four agents run at once.

CI for the batch (owner, 2026-11-22: "compressed ci time / minimal ci attempts"):
- Each merge passes the lead's local tiers as before; the Full run happens once per wave, dispatched on the wave's
  lead merge branch; main fast-forwards to that branch only when it is green, and is pushed once per wave.
- While the airport section is absent from a city, "existing cities unchanged" is shown by: archives
  byte-identical (`test:city` pins), the Freiburg gate hash, Munich 0.15 seed 1 in its four configurations and
  full-demand seed 1 in both signal modes with identical state hashes. Any difference brings back the full
  standing rule tables.
- Test-time budget per task: at most 20 s added to `npm run check` and 60 s per airport to `test:city`; full-day
  airport runs belong to the full traffic tier only. Agents report the time they add.
- Tests assert structure, never pixels; screenshots are evidence only.
- The nightly Full run skips a head commit that already has a green Full run.

Merge rule for the batch (lead, under the owner's standing rule): every existing city stays byte-identical
(archives and state hashes), since an airport is an optional section, or else meets the standing traffic rule;
each airport task meets its own gates (no two aircraft on a runway in conflict, no overlapping aircraft bodies,
no airside deadlock, the schedule's completion floor, the bundled-city load budget). A task that misses gets one
variant; a missing chain task that leaves every existing city unchanged merges with its gaps recorded in
`docs/plan.md`, so the batch does not stall, and any other miss parks.
