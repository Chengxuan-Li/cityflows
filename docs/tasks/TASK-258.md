## Goal

The owner chose (2026-11-20) a mixed slate of preloaded real places in the New city panel, alongside
Freiburg and the generated test layouts. This task does the first two, from extracts already committed
(no downloads): Munich Donnersbergerbrücke (`fixtures/osm/munich-donnersberger.osm`, terrain in
`fixtures/terrain/munich-donnersberger.json`) and Sindelfingen A81 (`fixtures/osm/sindelfingen-a81.osm`,
`fixtures/terrain/sindelfingen-a81.json`), and the machinery every later city reuses.

1. **One builder.** A bench script that builds every bundled real-place project from its committed
   extract (`bench/freiburg.ts` stays the Freiburg recipe, with its curation; the new cities ship the raw
   import, as the owner's 2026-11-20 rule asks: no per-id curation). Same import options as the cities'
   existing tests use, the default showcase scenario as Freiburg's, terrain if the 3D scene still builds
   within 5 s (else flat, as Freiburg, and say so).
2. **A staleness test.** A test that fails when a bundled project no longer matches a fresh build of its
   extract (hash of the rebuilt city against the archive), so an importer change cannot leave a bundled
   city stale unnoticed. It must be cheap enough for the tier it sits in, or live in `test:city`.
3. **The panel.** Group the New city panel into "Real places" (Freiburg, Munich, Sindelfingen, each with
   a one-line note of what it shows) and "Test layouts" (the generated scenarios). `?city=<id>` opens
   each. Keep it usable at phone width. A small thumbnail per real place is welcome if it stays under
   about 40 KB each and is generated by a script, not by hand.
4. **A showcase gate per new city.** At the default demand the city drains (TASK-154's all-roads criterion,
   against 900 s), nothing gridlocks, no stalled heads; production load to first frame, 2D and 3D frame
   budgets and 3D build time measured as `docs/decisions/2026-11-17-landing-city.md` does. A city that
   fails its gate is reported with the cause and left out of the panel, not curated.

## Scope

`bench/` (the builder; `bench/freiburg.ts` only to share code), `fixtures/projects/*.cityflows.json`
(new archives), `src/ui/bundled-projects.ts`, `src/ui/top-bar.tsx` and its styles, `src/main.tsx` if
