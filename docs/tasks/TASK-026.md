# TASK-026 — Large synthetic city fixture

Owner: unassigned
Gate: 2
Depends on: TASK-001 (and the merged sim for the acceptance run)

## Goal

A second bundled city, generated by code, large enough to stress the engine and to make a
convincing demo: arterials, local streets, one-ways, several neighborhoods and workplaces,
gateways, and a handful of signalized junctions. It is the workload for the performance
targets in architecture section 15 and the default "bigger city" in the New picker.

## In scope

- `src/fixtures/town.ts`: `town(seed = 1): City`, about 300 nodes. Arterial grid every
  400 m with two lanes per direction at 60 km/h; local streets every 100 m with one lane
  per direction at 30 km/h, a third of them one-way in alternating directions; two diagonal
  avenues; signals (two-phase programs through `buildCity.signal`) at every arterial
  crossing; six to eight residential places (about 6 000 residents in total), four work
  places (about 5 000 jobs), two retail places, one school, four gateway places at the
  arterial ends with population and jobs for external commuters. Every place has at least two
  connectors on different streets. Deterministic for a seed.
- Register it in `src/fixtures/scenarios.ts` (`FixtureId` 'town', a default scenario
  07:00 to 18:00) and in the fixture map the UI picker reads.
- `fixtures/projects/town.cityflows.json` generated the same way as the grid project
  (the `UPDATE_FIXTURES` test), with a scenario whose demand gives a visible rush.
- `tests/network/town.test.ts`: compiles with no errors and at most a handful of named
  warnings (list them); every place can reach every other place (routing from each `out`
  connector to each `in` connector succeeds).
- `tests/sim/town-day.test.ts` (tagged slow, skipped unless `RUN_SLOW=1`): a full scenario
  day headless completes with under 1 percent failed trips and no stranded vehicles; prints
  steps per second, peak driving and peak queued counts.

## Out of scope

- Any UI change (the picker is TASK-050's); any sim or routing change. If the town exposes
  an engine defect, report it with a minimal reproduction instead of working around it.

## Acceptance

- Both tests pass; the slow one is run once and its numbers are in the PR.
- The bundled project opens in the app and plays.
- `npm run check` green.

## Commands

`npm run check`, `RUN_SLOW=1 npm test -- tests/sim/town-day.test.ts`

## Deliverable

PR with the generator, registration, project fixture, tests and the measured numbers.
